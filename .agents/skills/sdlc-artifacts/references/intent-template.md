# Intent artifact guide

Use the repository root `intent.md`.

Recommended structure:

```markdown
---
status: draft
accepted_by:
accepted_at:
---

# Intent

## Problem
## Proposed outcome
## Affected users and systems
## Constraints
## Out of scope
## Success criteria
## Open questions
```

Keep intent focused on why the work exists, the desired outcome, and its boundaries. Avoid prematurely turning it into an implementation plan.
